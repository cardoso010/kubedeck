Kubernetes, at native speed.

kubedeck is a desktop client for your clusters, built with Tauri and Rust. Live lists, logs and terminals, Helm, metrics and Prometheus history, a Problems view that tells you what is broken, and guard rails for production. In English, Português, Español and 简体中文.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cardoso010/kubedeck/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Or download from the latest release.

What it does

Problems

Crash loops, OOMKilled, images that won't pull, unavailable workloads, full volumes and unhealthy nodes, worst first. One click opens the object.

Metrics without setup

CPU and memory against requests and limits, and Prometheus history found automatically through the API server, with your own credentials.

Command palette

⌘K to jump to any kind, CRD, resource, cluster or namespace, or to run an action.

Helm built in

Releases, history, rollback, upgrades and chart installs with a server-side dry run. Helm ships with the app.

Explain with Claude

On click only: the manifest (secrets removed), events and logs go to Claude, which suggests the cause and a fix.

Rightsizing

Each container's requests against real usage, and a suggestion you can apply to running pods in place, without a restart.

Nodes

Capacity, requests and usage, conditions and taints, cordon and drain with a preview of what gets evicted.

Storage

How full each volume is, its history, expansion, and moving it to a faster VolumeAttributesClass in place.

Dock

Terminals and logs that keep running while you browse, and several windows for several clusters.

Safe around production

  • Read-only by default. Contexts named prd, prod or production open locked, and the backend refuses every write until you unlock.
  • Type the name to confirm. Deleting, scaling, draining, rolling back or uninstalling on production asks for the object's name.
  • Secrets stay hidden. Secret values, and credentials in Helm values and manifests, are masked until you reveal them, and Secrets are never sent to Claude.
  • Your credentials, your RBAC. Everything goes through your kubeconfig, including EKS and GKE exec plugins. No agents in the cluster, no telemetry.
  • Nothing runs behind your back. The app asks before running a login command for the first time, and locking a window closes its terminals.
  • Signed updates. The app updates itself in one click and refuses packages without a valid signature.
